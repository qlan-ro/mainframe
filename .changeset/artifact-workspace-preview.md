Show Claude artifacts in the workspace: artifact cards get a clean row with an Open button, and artifact links open in Mainframe. The daemon downloads each artifact through the CLI, with no model or claude.ai sign-in, and shows it in a sandbox. Docs, Dashboards and Design Systems open on claude.ai. Inspect picks elements inside a design to send to the agent, open tabs update after a publish, and `/design` and `/slides` now work in chats.
