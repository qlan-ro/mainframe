A permission request, a question, or a plan awaiting your answer now sits above
the composer instead of at the end of the transcript, so it stays in view while
you scroll back through the run it is blocking. The pinned slot takes at most
55% of the thread and scrolls inside itself, so a long plan never pushes the
composer off screen. Answering it returns the space to the transcript.
