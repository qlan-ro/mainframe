The composer is one card that carries its context: project and branch above it in a new session, ongoing work above it after that, and where the session runs, its branch and its changes below. While a turn runs, a message can be queued or steered into the turn (Settings › Chat picks which one Enter does), Stop ends the turn and its sub-agents, and messages a stop held back wait in the composer. Requests from sub-agents and delegated tasks show above the composer on their own, oldest first, and Unattended asks once per session. Picking another provider's model switches the chat without a confirmation. “Always allow” lists everything it saves and says what it leaves out: a permission mode wider than Auto-Edits, or a suggestion the app cannot read, is never saved from a prompt, whichever client answers. The command a request runs shows whole, cannot be allowed before its end was shown, and what is approved keeps its stored order whatever its script.
