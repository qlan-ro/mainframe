The composer is one card that carries its context: project and branch above it in a new session, ongoing work above it after that, and where the session runs, its branch and its changes below. While a turn runs, a message can be queued or steered into the turn (Settings › Chat picks which one Enter does), Stop ends the turn and its sub-agents, and messages a stop held back wait in the composer. Requests from sub-agents and delegated tasks show above the composer on their own, oldest first, and Unattended asks once per session. Picking another provider's model switches the chat without a confirmation.
