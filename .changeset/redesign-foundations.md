Quieter errors, clearer focus, and no scrollbar tracks

- Failed work (tools, runs, messages, error labels) is a muted rose instead of red. Red is kept for what can't be undone, Stop and limits.
- Keyboard focus is one solid blue line everywhere, with no glow.
- Scrollbars are thin overlay thumbs that appear while an area scrolls or is hovered, with no track and no gutter, whatever the macOS scroll bar setting.
