A new session opens on its project: where it will run, the three sessions you used last, and open tasks to start from. Picking a task adds it to the message under anything you typed. Files, images, preview captures and code annotations sit in the composer as tiles of one size, with notes you edit in place. Captures and the editor's “Add Agent Context” now join the message you are writing instead of being sent or quoted at once, and quotes say which message they are from. The editor’s menu keeps a way to quote `path:line`, as “Quote Reference”. If a new session’s first message fails, the composer says why and lets you restore it, copy its text or discard it. Switching a draft’s project, worktree or daemon keeps your text and quotes, removes the captures, code annotations and session mentions of the old place, and offers Undo.
