The session panel is a quieter index of the session. Choose which sections it shows from its new Sections menu. Every activity row leads with what it is and ends with its state: click an agent or a delegated task to open it, unfold a workflow to follow its phases, or click a monitor, background command or schedule to go to its row in the transcript. On a narrow window the panel floats above the composer, so you can keep typing. The branch, worktree and changes moved to a strip under the composer.
