Forks, side chats, delegated tasks, sub-agents and any session you want beside the conversation now open as workspace tabs, and the chat column always holds one conversation. Closing a chat tab keeps the chat and its unsent message. Split views and open side chats become workspace tabs when you upgrade. The workspace slides in beside the chat, and closing its last tab closes it.
