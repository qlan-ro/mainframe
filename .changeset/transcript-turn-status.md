The turn's live status moved from above the composer into the transcript, as in the Claude and Codex desktop apps. A status row at the end of the turn shows the phase (Thinking, Writing, Working, Needs your input, Stopping, Compacting), the elapsed time and running background tasks. When an activity group or a running tool ends the transcript, its header shows the time and tasks instead, so each turn has a single live indicator. The separate "Compacting…" pill is gone.
