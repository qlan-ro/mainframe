Fade crowded session tab titles instead of wrapping

Session tab titles wrap onto a second line when the strip gets crowded. Keep them on one line and fade them out at the right edge, the same way sidebar rows do.

I'll find where the tab renders its title, then switch it to the same fade the sidebar rows use.

in packages/ui/src/features/session-tabs

The title is a plain span with break-words, so a narrow pill wraps it. FadeLabel already measures the clip and only fades when the text overflows, so a short title keeps full ink.

52- <span className="text-xs leading-4 break-words">{title}</span>
52+ <FadeLabel className="text-xs leading-4" tooltipDelay={600}>
53+ {title}
54+ </FadeLabel>
