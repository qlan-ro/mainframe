## 2.17.0

### Minor Changes

- [#987](https://github.com/qlan-ro/mainframe/pull/987) [`2e85b7f`](https://github.com/qlan-ro/mainframe/commit/2e85b7ff10b76ffaca16373a2faba5d8a48b165c) Thanks [@doruchiulan](https://github.com/doruchiulan)! - Add a preview setting, Settings › Chat › "Mentions and commands as chips", off by default. With it on, the composer shows `@` and `/` tokens as atomic chips: the caret steps over one in a single press, Backspace deletes it whole, and undo, copy and paste keep it a chip. Tokens typed by hand become chips once finished, and quote segments and queued-message edits show chips too. The text sent to the agent does not change.

- [#955](https://github.com/qlan-ro/mainframe/pull/955) [`e65ea9b`](https://github.com/qlan-ro/mainframe/commit/e65ea9ba81bb03e98045579f37577353db4dae3e) Thanks [@doruchiulan](https://github.com/doruchiulan)! - Align the window palette, usage capsule, session row states, floating session panel, composer, gates, and transcript with the window redesign.

- [#1007](https://github.com/qlan-ro/mainframe/pull/1007) [`a4da0b0`](https://github.com/qlan-ro/mainframe/commit/a4da0b060d669fc17be95752ef8c8aa2502c5df4) Thanks [@doruchiulan](https://github.com/doruchiulan)! - Add a preview setting, Settings › Chat › "Plain transcript list", off by default. With it on, every loaded message is a plain row under one scroll owner with browser scroll anchoring and no virtualizer, the newest rows mount first, and older pages land without moving what you are reading, including when you flick to the top. The top of the list shows a spinner with "Loading earlier messages" while a page loads, a cue while more is above, and "Beginning of conversation" at the start. The transcript no longer shows a scrollbar thumb in either list, and a chat that is still loading shows its spinner only if the load takes longer than 200 ms.

- [#946](https://github.com/qlan-ro/mainframe/pull/946) [`3724775`](https://github.com/qlan-ro/mainframe/commit/3724775b16b9e983ec6241ce800c71ddabd78c33) Thanks [@doruchiulan](https://github.com/doruchiulan)! - The composer is one card that carries its context: project and branch above it in a new session, ongoing work above it after that, and where the session runs, its branch and its changes below. While a turn runs, a message can be queued or steered into the turn (Settings › Chat picks which one Enter does), Stop ends the turn and its sub-agents, and messages a stop held back wait in the composer. Requests from sub-agents and delegated tasks show above the composer on their own, oldest first, and Unattended asks once per session. Picking another provider's model switches the chat without a confirmation. “Always allow” lists everything it saves and says what it leaves out: a permission mode wider than Auto-Edits, or a suggestion the app cannot read, is never saved from a prompt, whichever client answers. The command a request runs shows whole, cannot be allowed before its end was shown, and what is approved keeps its stored order whatever its script.

- [#941](https://github.com/qlan-ro/mainframe/pull/941) [`0ab667e`](https://github.com/qlan-ro/mainframe/commit/0ab667e4a7c7d40b7bb23c5c145088792d999e95) Thanks [@doruchiulan](https://github.com/doruchiulan)! - Quieter errors, clearer focus, and no scrollbar tracks

  - Failed work (tools, runs, messages, error labels) is a muted rose instead of red. Red is kept for what can't be undone, Stop and limits.
  - Keyboard focus is one solid blue line everywhere, with no glow.
  - Scrollbars are thin overlay thumbs that appear while an area scrolls or is hovered, with no track and no gutter, whatever the macOS scroll bar setting.

- [#953](https://github.com/qlan-ro/mainframe/pull/953) [`320e396`](https://github.com/qlan-ro/mainframe/commit/320e3962060077c7d6626675edde383456a24b31) Thanks [@doruchiulan](https://github.com/doruchiulan)! - A new session opens on its project: where it will run, the three sessions you used last, and open tasks to start from. Picking a task adds it to the message under anything you typed. Files, images, preview captures and code annotations sit in the composer as tiles of one size, with notes you edit in place. Captures and the editor's “Add Agent Context” now join the message you are writing instead of being sent or quoted at once, and quotes say which message they are from. The editor’s menu keeps a way to quote `path:line`, as “Quote Reference”. If a new session’s first message fails, the composer says why and lets you restore it, copy its text or discard it. Switching a draft’s project, worktree or daemon keeps your text and quotes, removes the captures, code annotations and session mentions of the old place, and offers Undo.

- [#942](https://github.com/qlan-ro/mainframe/pull/942) [`9bbd816`](https://github.com/qlan-ro/mainframe/commit/9bbd816975579c954ab2d03cff199d2158201ba1) Thanks [@doruchiulan](https://github.com/doruchiulan)! - The session panel is a quieter index of the session. Choose which sections it shows from its new Sections menu. Every activity row leads with what it is and ends with its state: click an agent or a delegated task to open it, unfold a workflow to follow its phases, or click a monitor, background command or schedule to go to its row in the transcript. On a narrow window the panel floats above the composer, so you can keep typing. The branch, worktree and changes moved to a strip under the composer.

- [#944](https://github.com/qlan-ro/mainframe/pull/944) [`38640de`](https://github.com/qlan-ro/mainframe/commit/38640de9fca8fb8892623596269ea6a41dba2aa7) Thanks [@doruchiulan](https://github.com/doruchiulan)! - Redesigned session list: each row is one line with the project's letter, the title and one value on the right (a spinner while it runs, "needs you" when it waits on you, or the time). Filter, sort and more appear when you hover the first group header, and you can now filter the list to running sessions or sessions that need you.

- [#948](https://github.com/qlan-ro/mainframe/pull/948) [`7de33a7`](https://github.com/qlan-ro/mainframe/commit/7de33a7418e4773e4614cc173a07f4c9310fe032) Thanks [@doruchiulan](https://github.com/doruchiulan)! - Redesigned transcript

  - Each turn has one work row ("Worked for 2m 14s · edited 3 files"). It folds when the turn ends and opens on rows, one per step. The answer stays in view.
  - A failed step is counted in the work row, which opens on it. A step that a later one made good reads "1 failed, retried" in grey.
  - Agents, delegated tasks and workflows hold their turn open with a live count until they finish. A delegated task's result appears in its own row. A live one has Stop in its row.
  - A turn that waits on a permission reads "Waiting on you" and stays open. A turn claims an end only when its own messages say one; otherwise it reads "Worked" with a dot, which is how Claude history reads after a reload. A step that never came back reads "No result recorded", and "Stopped" only where something says so.
  - Skills, plans, schedules, monitors, worktrees and notifications are pills that stay visible when the work folds. An answered question stays as a small card.
  - User messages and the turn’s reply footer show their time on hover or focus. Messages in the middle of a turn lose their footer times. A time marker appears at the start and after a 30-minute gap.
  - Links share one style and open where Settings › General › "Open links in" says: the workspace browser or the system's. A `0.0.0.0` address counts as a local server.
  - A running background command's output is readable in its row.
  - Settings › General › Transcript (Verbose/Compact) became "Finished work" (Fold/Keep open). A stored Verbose choice starts as Keep open, Compact as Fold.
  - Code in the transcript is syntax-highlighted again.
