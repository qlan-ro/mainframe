# Chat area notes

Read this when changing one of the areas below. The ownership rules are in the "assistant-ui boundary" section of [`../CLAUDE.md`](../CLAUDE.md); these notes record how each area is built today and the decisions behind it.

## assistant-ui areas

- **Composer.** The native pieces are `ComposerPrimitive` Root, Input, AddAttachment, AttachmentDropzone and Attachments. Send, Stop and the Queue / Steer split are our own buttons (`features/chat/composer/send/SendControls.tsx`). Queued messages are daemon-held and drawn by `composer/queue/QueuedRows.tsx`; assistant-ui's local `Queue` is a different model, so do not use it. The config toolbar (`composer/config-toolbar/`: model, effort and features in one menu, plan, permission, worktree) is stateless and **PATCHes the daemon directly**: `setChatConfig` (model, plan, permission) and `setChatTuning` (effort, features) in `lib/api/chats.ts`, not `composer.setRunConfig`, which targets the AI-SDK transport and does nothing under our external-store runtime. **Config is server-authoritative with no optimistic edits:** the controller seeds it from REST and mirrors every `chat.updated` into `state.chatConfig`; `useComposerTuning` reads it live, resolves provider inheritance through `useProviderDefaults` (`config-toolbar/use-provider-defaults.ts`, passed to `ModelMenu` as `providerDefaults`), and a control only fires the PATCH. **Pickers:** `/` and `@` run on our own trigger engine (`components/trigger-engine/`), wired in `composer/triggers/ComposerTriggers.tsx`. `ComposerPrimitive.Unstable_TriggerPopoverRoot` stays mounted with no children only because it is the public mounter of the input-plugin registry that routes `ComposerPrimitive.Input`'s keys to our field; it is an `Unstable_` API, so keep the coupling in that file. `/` lists daemon slash commands, then skills (`SkillsProvider`, `features/skills/use-chat-skills.tsx`); commands lead so a handful stay visible above hundreds of skills, and the send path re-matches the invocation through `commands/command-registry.ts` to attach `metadata.command`. `@` lists agents, sessions and project files (debounced `searchFiles` into a cache the sync adapter reads), and drills into `@dir/` project paths or `@/` and `@~` filesystem paths. Insertion writes plain `/name` and `@path` text the CLI parses, not directive chips (`triggers/directive-formatter.ts`). **Never `useComposer()`** (getSnapshot loop); use `useAuiState` and `useAui().composer`. **New-thread flow is draft-aware:** the picker is skipped when a project is in scope, the config controls work on the `__LOCALID_*` draft, and the chat is created on the first send only, so there are no empty sessions.
- **Quote and select-to-quote.** Only `SelectionToolbarPrimitive.Root` is native (`components/ui/assistant-ui/quote.tsx`, hand-ported from the assistant-ui registry). `features/chat/thread/ChatSelectionToolbar.tsx` reads the selection itself and appends to the multi-quote segment store (`composer/segments/`), and `SegmentQuotePill` draws each quote. The native single-quote composer state (`ComposerPrimitive.Quote`, `QuoteText`, `QuoteDismiss`) and `MessagePrimitive.Quote` are not used; see the docstrings of those two files for why.
- **Sessions list.** One global `useRemoteThreadListRuntime` (all sessions; domain data in the thread's `custom`: projectId, tags, prState, worktree, status, pinned) with native `ThreadListItemPrimitive` rows rendered inside our grouped, filtered sidebar through `ThreadListItemByIndexProvider` (`features/sessions/SessionRowItemScope.tsx`). Not the flat default `ThreadListPrimitive.Items`, and not per-project runtimes. Archive stays native: the worktree-deletion choice is resolved inside the adapter's `archive` through `ArchiveWorktreeDialog`, mounted once in `app/AppShell.tsx`, and throwing on cancel rolls back assistant-ui's optimistic archive. Pin, rename, archive and copy-id live in `SessionContextMenu`; the Tags action opens `TagPopover` through `sessions/tags/use-tag-popover-target.ts` and a single `TagPopoverHost`. Controllers stay in the registry across switches, but the WS subscription is gated to the active thread (`ChatWsSubscription`; a `__LOCALID_*` chat never subscribes). A new session goes through `new-thread-coordinator.createForLocal`: the id stays `__LOCALID_*` for life and `controller.setRemoteId` stamps the remote id. **Grouping:** `arrangeSessions` (`sessions/view-model/group-sessions.ts`) with the `SessionSortMenu` modes Recent activity, Name (A–Z), Status and Project; pinned sessions come first in each. **Project scope:** `ScopeStrip` shows the projects as stacked avatars; any number can be in scope and the list shows their union, an empty scope is "All projects", and toggling never activates a session. Activating a session outside the scope clears it (`sessions/ws/use-session-list-router.ts`). **Rows are one line:** project letter, title, then one trailing value (a spinner, "needs you" or the time; `view-model/session-status.ts`). Everything else lives in the `SessionMetaCard` shown on hover or keyboard focus (`use-session-meta-card.ts`). Boot auto-selects the most recent session, so the app never lands on an empty thread.
- **Window chrome.** A 48px `TitleBar` on `bg-sidebar` spans the window and is the one `data-drag-region`. Its segments stand on the column edges below: traffic lights and the usage capsule, `SessionTabs`, the workspace tabs with `+` and Files (`TitleBarWorkspace`, only while the workspace shows), then Search and the Chat / Workspace toggles. **Edges are measured, never computed:** a column reports its left edge through `useColumnEdge` (`layout/column-edges.ts`) and draws its hairline on the pixel just left of it. Below it sit the 56px `NavRail` (Chats, Tasks, Automations, Setup Advisor) and one `ContentCard` holding the `AppSidebar` view host and the chat. The chat column holds one conversation under `ChatColumnHeader`. Every secondary chat (fork, side chat, delegated task, sub-agent, another session) opens as a workspace tab (`features/workspace-chat/`). The session panel (`features/session-panel/`) docks as a 300px sibling once the column is at least 1044px wide and overlays below that (`panel-mode.ts`). The sidebar defaults to `SIDEBAR_WIDTH` (260px); its dragged width and `sidebarView` persist in `store/ui-prefs.ts`. The session panel's Context section keeps the in-session file mentions (`derive-session-items.ts`, `ContextFileItem.tsx`). Agents are not listed in the panel; they appear only in the `@` picker.

**Framing:** assistant-ui's runtime-integration hooks (`ModelContext.register`, `useThreadTokenUsage`, `useAssistantInstructions`) do nothing under our external-store runtime, because they target the AI-SDK transport. Its presentational pieces work when fed our data, for example `useMessageTiming` in `MessageFooter`, and the `SyntaxHighlighter` and `CodeHeader` slots of `MarkdownTextPrimitive` fed our shiki engine (`features/chat/parts/markdown-text.tsx`).

## Mainframe areas that still use assistant-ui pieces

- **Turns and tool rows → Mainframe's.** A turn spans several messages (a compaction, a loaded skill or a delivered task result can fall between its parts, and the provider can start a turn with no user message), so `view-model/turn` groups the messages and `messages/turn/TurnSlice.tsx` draws one message's share of its turn. The slice iterates the parts itself, and `ToolRow` dispatches on `toolKind`; `MessagePrimitive.GroupedParts`, `tools.by_name` and assistant-ui's tool cards are not used. The row primitives still come from assistant-ui (`ThreadPrimitive.MessageByIndex`, `MessagePrimitive.Root`, `ActionBarPrimitive`, `ReadonlyThreadProvider` for nested transcripts), and text renders through `TextMessagePartProvider` + `MarkdownTextPrimitive`. Row presentation is moving onto shadcn Trace and tool output onto Code Block.
- **Permissions, questions and plans → inline gate cards fed by our `extras`.** assistant-ui's tool-call `approval` gate (`respondToApproval`, backed by the external-store adapter's `onRespondToToolApproval`) does work under `useExternalStoreRuntime`; we bypass it by choice because our gate data arrives out of band (`control_request` → `extras.permissions`), not in the message stream. Three prop-driven cards (`PermissionGate`, `AskUserQuestionGate`, `PlanGate`) under `features/chat/gates/` are dispatched by `ControlRequest.toolName` from one `ChatGateMount` in the thread's sticky footer above the composer (`ChatThreadViewport.tsx`; a plain div, not `ThreadPrimitive.ViewportFooter`). The cards sit in a height-capped, internally scrolling slot (`chat-thread-gate-slot`) so a pending gate never scrolls out of reach (#336). `use-gate-queue.ts` collects every waiting request, the chat's own, its sub-agents' and its delegated tasks', sorted by `askedAt`; one gate shows at a time and replies go through `replyToPermission`. Permission cards dismiss on answer; question and plan answers persist through the tool-result cards. The gate renders the daemon's option list **verbatim** (`chat-permission-option-{optionId}`, styled by `kind`, never by id or label; spec decision 12). Unknown kinds render neutrally and answer as deny (decision 25). **Don't invent** risk levels, scopes or per-argument toggles. **Reply seam:** `replyToPermission` / `ReplyFn` take the `ControlResponse`, which carries its own `requestId`, plus the clicked option's `optionId`; plan and question answers omit the option and the session plane synthesizes one. **Lost answers self-heal through resume:** answering drops the gate optimistically, and if the answer dies with the socket, the facade's `session/resume` redelivers the still-open gate under the same correlation id.
- **Markdown, reasoning and parts.** Text renders through `MarkdownTextPrimitive` (`parts/markdown-text.tsx`). Reasoning is a collapsed "Thought" row in the turn (`ThoughtRow` in `messages/turn/TextUnit.tsx`, drawing `parts/ReasoningText.tsx`), not assistant-ui's `Reasoning` block.
